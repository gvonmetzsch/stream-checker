Settings

        
      


      

        Claude scene check
        
Optional. With a key, each check sends one small frame (about 512 px) straight from this phone to api.anthropic.com and Claude says whether it is flowing water, a lake, a puddle, or not water at all. The key is stored only on this phone and never leaves it except for that one request. Without it, the app assumes it is looking at water.

Help

        
      


      
How to hold the phone

      
    
        
  • Portrait, with the top of the phone pointing across the water in the direction you are looking.
    • 
        
  • Tilt it down at moving water, roughly 30 to 60 degrees. Flat or upright poses confuse the compass.
    • 
        
  • Hold still for the whole 4 seconds. Panning looks like flow.
    • 
        
  • Fill the frame with the water surface. Avoid glare, reflections and the bank.
    • 
      


      
What the verdicts mean

      

        
Right direction
The water moves within 60° of the mapped flow of the nearest stream, river or canal. OpenStreetMap draws waterways downstream, so the map knows which way is down.

        
Wrong way
The measured flow is 120° or more from the mapped direction. Check the hold, the compass, and whether you filmed an eddy, then enjoy the moment.

        
Sideways
Between the two: usually the phone pointed along the bank rather than across the current.

        
Terrain estimate
No mapped waterway within 200 m, so the app checked whether the ground drops along the measured bearing using 90 m elevation data. Coarse.

        
Unverified
The flow was measured but the map could not be reached and no saved data covers this spot.

        
Lake, puddle, tidal, still
Still water and tidal water are exempt. Lakes do not go anywhere.

        
Not water
Only the optional Claude scene check can tell. Without it the app assumes water.

        
Can't see detail, hold still, no compass
Too little texture (glare, flat water, too far), too much camera motion, or no usable heading. Try again.

      


      
Readouts

      
Bearings are true compass bearings with 16-point cardinal abbreviations. Consistency is the mean resultant length of the per-frame flow vectors (1.0 is perfectly steady). Coverage is the share of the frame with enough texture to track. The channel distance is the perpendicular distance to the mapped line.


      
Limits

      
    
        
  • Wind ripples, cloud reflections and standing waves can fool the flow estimate.
    • 
        
  • Small creeks are often missing from OpenStreetMap; then the terrain estimate or Unverified applies.
    • 
        
  • Android compasses need the occasional figure-8 calibration and dislike nearby metal.
    • 
        
  • Public Overpass servers are sometimes overloaded. Save streams near you while you have signal.
    • 
        
  • On iOS, denying the motion prompt sticks until the site data is cleared.
    •
